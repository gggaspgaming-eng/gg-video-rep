Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Gasp! Gaming is a personal publishing tool used by one person — the owner of the Gasp! Gaming channel — to publish their own videos. It is not a service offered to other people, and it has no users other than its owner.

What the tool does

The tool runs locally on the owner's own computer. It assembles a video from material the owner has written, recorded and edited, and then uploads that video to the owner's own accounts on video platforms, including TikTok.

What data is collected

None from anyone other than the owner. The tool has no sign-up, no accounts, no visitors and no analytics. It does not collect, store, process or transmit personal data belonging to any third party.

Credentials

To upload on the owner's behalf, the tool stores the access and refresh tokens issued by each platform in a configuration file on the owner's own computer. Those tokens are never transmitted anywhere except back to the platform that issued them, are never shared with third parties, and can be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings, which immediately ends the tool's access.

What is shared with third parties

Nothing, other than the video the owner chooses to publish, sent to the platform the owner chooses to publish it on.

Retention

Videos and configuration live on the owner's computer for as long as the owner keeps them. There is no server, no database in the cloud, and no backup held by anyone else.
